How to read this report
A 100% sum alone does not prove a partition: timers may overlap, omit work, or use different scopes. Verify nesting in the solver version used by the job. No values are clipped or normalized to force 100%. Negative category values are retained and flagged.
Gap = max(total − tracked, 0); excess = max(tracked − total, 0). Net residual = total − tracked. Missing or zero totals are excluded from aggregates and distributions. Missing category values follow the selected policy; omitted zero stats and interrupted output cannot be distinguished automatically. “Unreported” does not imply timeout.
