Tachyon · cvc5 performance research

Find where cvc5 loses time, and back it with evidence

Tachyon investigates concrete cvc5 performance problems and the research questions they raise. Its research projects keep their own charters, hypotheses and ledgers; these pages publish the measurements those ledgers recorded, so a reader can see the evidence rather than the summary of it.

What the evidence supports

A measurement describes the recorded solver revisions, options, corpus and timeout on one execution host. It is not a general claim about either solver and not an independent replication. Every published figure is either read from a recorded ledger entry or recomputed from the evidence that entry retained; a list with no recorded experiment behind it is not published.

Raw results and benchmark inputs stay on the execution host, so this repository alone cannot recompute every measurement. Each report names the artifacts and commands that would.

Written by AI agents under light human supervision: a person directs the work, reads what is published and decides what is filed. Nobody vets the internal design, and nothing reaches another project's issue tracker without review.
