# 2026-09-14 — the baseline: z3, cvc5 default, cvc5 best known

**Goal 1, the gap.** Three solvers configurations on the whole set at 30 s,
same host, one after another, never overlapping. Plus one cross-check on the
z3 version, and one recorded misstep.

## 1. What was asked

Goal 0 and goal 1 of [`README.md`](../README.md): fix the set and the
baselines, then measure the gap. No row of the register is tested here; this
is the denominator everything else divides by.

## 5. What it settled

- **The gap is real and large on this set**: a factor of 4.3 on PAR2 for the
  best known cvc5 configuration, 1049 benchmarks of 6124 in the gap set, and
  545 that cvc5 cannot do in 30 s at all while z3 can. These are the two
  numbers of `README.md`, "How we would know it is working", as of today.
- **The best known configuration is worth a lot and is not enough.** Against
  default it removes 83 timeouts, halves the time on solved benchmarks
  (11030 s to 6202 s) and cuts the gap set from 2266 to 1049. That is the
  register's `--no-cbqi` and `--user-pat=strict` rows measured together; it
  leaves a factor of 4.3.
- **The `slow/` subset is essentially solved**, 137 of 138, and only 14 of
  them are still ≥ 10× slower; the gap now lives in `sundance/` and
  `verus-no-option/`, and roughly half of it is timeouts and half is
  solved-but-slow. Goal 2 has two populations to attribute, not one.
- **No soundness issue**: no `sat`, no disagreement, in any run.

## 6. What it did not settle, and the misstep

- **The oracle's options decide the oracle.** The first z3 run, z3 4.8.17
  with no options, "timed out" on 430 benchmarks, 124 of them in `slow/`
  where the benchmark names say z3 4.12.5 took a fraction of a second. On one
  of them, both z3 4.8.17 and 4.15.4 without options ran past two minutes,
  and with the Verus options both answered in 0.25 s. The z3 baseline is
  therefore *z3 as Verus runs it*. The results file of the misstep is kept in
  [`data/`](data/) as `results-z3_solve.sh-quant-091426.txt` and is not a
  baseline for anything.
- **The z3 version matters a little, the options a lot.** 4.8.17 with the
  same options: 5966 solved, 130 timeouts, PAR2 13466 — a 1.33× PAR2 ratio
  against 4.15.4 (76 benchmarks only 4.15.4 solves, 28 only 4.8.17 solves),
  against a 3.0× ratio between 4.8.17 with and without options. The oracle is
  4.15.4 from here on; the 4.8.17 file is in [`data/`](data/) for anyone who
  wants to check.
- **cvc5 is run as this project runs it, not as Verus runs it.** Verus passes
  cvc5 options of its own; none were used here, and whether its
  configuration differs from `--no-cbqi --user-pat=strict` is not known to
  this entry. Worth one run.
- **30 s hides everything past 30 s.** A run at a long timeout (goal 1's
  row 5 in the latency table) would say how many of the 545 are slow rather
  than stuck. Not done.
- **Tooling observations**, for whoever runs the next one: a finished tmux
  window blocks the next launch until closed with `submit -k NAME`; a job
  name with dots in it cannot be addressed by `tmux capture-pane -t
  session:name`, so watch the results file or the driver process instead.
